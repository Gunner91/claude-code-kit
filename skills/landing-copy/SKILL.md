description: Writes conversion-focused landing page copy (hero, benefits, objections, FAQ, call to action) from a short product brief. Use for marketing pages, product launches or service pages.
argument-hint: "[product/service brief]"
---

Brief: $ARGUMENTS

Before writing, ask at most 3 short questions if any of these is unknown: who the customer is, the main result they want, the price or offer.

Then write the page in the language of the brief:
1. **Hero**: headline (the outcome, max 10 words), subheadline (how + for whom), primary CTA.
2. **Problem**: 3 pains in the customer's own words.
3. **Solution**: how it works in 3 steps.
4. **Benefits**: 4-6 benefits as outcomes, not features.
5. **Proof**: placeholders for real numbers, cases and reviews — never invent testimonials or statistics.
6. **Offer & pricing** block.
7. **Objections → FAQ**: 5 real objections with honest answers.
8. **Final CTA** with a reason to act now (only if true).

Style: concrete, short sentences, no clichés ("innovative", "cutting-edge", "unlock").
